🛠️ Technical Details (For The Curious)

laya-ultrafast is built with Python and uses a revolutionary "indexed action space" approach. This means it's pre-programmed with a smart catalog of browser actions (click, type, scroll, navigate) and knows exactly when to use each one - making it incredibly fast and efficient.

It supports local LLM models, meaning the artificial intelligence that controls the browser runs on your own hardware. No internet connection needed for AI processing. This is what we mean by "local-first" and "self-hosted" - you have complete ownership and control.
